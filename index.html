04 — About

Behind
The Cut.

I'm Malek Ahmed, a video editor and content creator based in Egypt, working with brands, founders, and creators worldwide. For the past five years I've cut everything from 15-second product reels to 40-minute documentaries — and the goal has never changed: make the idea land.

My process starts with the story, not the timeline. I look for the moment that earns attention, build the rhythm around it, then finish with colour, sound and motion that serve the message. Clear briefs, honest timelines, and a first cut you'll actually want to watch.

05 — Services

What I
Deliver.

Four ways to work together. Every service lists exactly what you get and how long it takes — no surprises, no vague “premium packages.”

Scope first. Then speed.

01Service 1

Short-form editing

Vertical cuts for Reels, TikTok and Shorts, built to hold attention from the first frame.

  • 15–60 s vertical edits
  • Captions and on-screen text
  • Sound design and music sync
  • Exports in 9:16, 1:1 and 4:5
  • Two revision rounds

Turnaround

48–72 hours per video

02Service 2

Long-form & YouTube

Full edits for YouTube videos, interviews and podcasts, from raw footage to upload-ready master.

  • Multicam sync and pacing cut
  • Colour correction and audio mix
  • B-roll, graphics and captions
  • Three thumbnail options
  • Chapters and end screen

Turnaround

5–7 days per 15 minutes of final runtime

03Service 3

Commercial & brand films

Product launches, campaign spots and brand stories cut to a brief and a deadline.

  • Cut from script or shot list
  • Colour grade and sound mix
  • Licensed music selection
  • Master plus platform cutdowns
  • Motion titles and end card

Turnaround

2–3 weeks from footage handover

04Service 4

Motion graphics

Animated elements that explain, brand or punctuate a video.

  • Logo animations and reveals
  • Lower thirds and title packages
  • Kinetic typography
  • Animated infographics
  • Source project files included

Turnaround

3–5 days per 30 seconds of animation
